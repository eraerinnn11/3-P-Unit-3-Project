Recipe #1: Tiramisu

  • Time:
    Mixing time- ~45 minutes
    Setting time- 1+ hours
  • Serves: About 12 people
Ingredients Quantity
Heavy Cream 1 1/2 Cup (360 ml)
Mascarpone Cheese 1 container (8 ounces)
Granulated Sugar 1/3 cup (67g)
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp (5ml)
Epresso (to each their own but about 3 pods)
Ladyfinger cookies 1 package

Step 1: Add heavy cream to a mixing bowl and beat on medium speed with electric mixers (or use a stand mixer). Slowly add sugar and vanilla and continue to beat until stiff peaks. Add mascarpone cheese and fold in until combined. Set aside.

Step 2: Add coffee to a shallow bowl. Dip the lady fingers in the coffee (Don't soak them–just quickly dip them on both sides to get them wet) and lay them in a single layer on the bottom of an 8×8'' or similar size pan.

Step 3: Smooth half of the mascarpone mixture over the top. Add another layer of dipped lady fingers. Smooth remaining mascarpone cream over the top.

Step 4: Put the pan in the fridge for at least one hour (more time is recommended for a desireable texture). After chilling for one hour or more, dust a generous amount of cocoa powder on the tiramisu so that it covers the entire top.

Recipe #2: Tres Leches

  • Time:
    mixing/cooking time= 1 1/2 hours
    setting time= 5+ hours
  • Serves: About 18-20 people
Ingredients Quantity
All-Purpose flour 1 cup (120g)
Baking Soda 1 1/2 Teaspoons (6g)
Salt 1/4 Tsp
Eggs 5
Granulated Sugar 1 cup (200g)
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp (5ml)
Whole Milk 1/3 cup (80ml)
Evaporated Milk 1 can (354ml)
Condensed Milk 1 can (396 ml)

Step 1: Add ¾ cup sugar to the bowl with the egg yolks and mix on high speed until yolks are pale yellow. Add ⅓ cup milk and vanilla and stir to combine. Pour the egg yolk mixture over the flour mixture and stir gently just until combined (don’t over-mix)

Step 2: Use electric beaters to beat the egg whites on high speed. As the begin to whip into stiff peaks, gradually mix in the remaining ¼ cup of sugar. Fold the stiffly beaten egg whites into the batter gently, scraping the bottom and sides of the bowl, until combined.

Step 3: Pour batter into pan and smooth it into an even layer. Bake at 350 degrees F. for 25 to 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center of the cake comes out clean. Remove from the oven and allow cake to cool completely.

Step 4: Combine the evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk, and whole milk in a small bowl. Once the cake has cooled use a fork to poke holes all over the top of the cake.

Step 5: Slowly pour the milk mixture over the the top of the cake, making sure to pour near the edges and all around. Refrigerate the cake for at least 1 hour or overnight, to allow it to soak up the milk.

Step 6: In the meantime, whip the heavy cream, sugar and vanilla until stiff peaks. Smooth over the top of the cake.

Step 7: Serve with a sprinkle of cinnamon on top, and fresh sliced strawberries, if desired. Store Tres Leches Cake in the refrigerator, covered, for 5 hours or overnight.
